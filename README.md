# Consumer-De-influencing-AI-insight-engine
**Live Website:** [Explore the Consumer De-Influencing Insight Engine](https://benevolent-fudge-e0c3a.netlify.app/)
# About the Project: 
The Consumer De-Influencing Insight Engine is a postgraduate Consumer Behaviour research 
prototype that attempts to identify and interpret consumer conversations in which people discourage, 
question, or resist a purchase. The project is designed around the idea that consumer influence is not 
only positive persuasion: consumers can also influence one another through warnings, regret, 
scepticism, negative experiences, price-value comparisons and anti-consumption statements. 
# Business Objective:
To help brand managers understand consumer dissatisfaction, purchase resistance and brand perception, translating consumer insights into actionable marketing strategies.

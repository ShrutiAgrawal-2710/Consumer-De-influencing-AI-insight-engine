# Consumer De-influencing AI-insight engine
**Live Website:** [Explore the Consumer De-Influencing Insight Engine](https://benevolent-fudge-ce0c3a.netlify.app/)
# About the Project: 
The Consumer De-Influencing Insight Engine is a consumer behaviour research prototype designed to identify and interpret consumer conversations in which individuals discourage, question, or resist a purchase.

The project explores the concept of consumer de-influencing, where consumers influence one another through warnings, regret, scepticism, negative experiences, price-value comparisons, and anti-consumption narratives.

The platform aims to transform consumer discussions into meaningful insights that help brands understand purchase resistance and evolving consumer perceptions. 
# Business Objective:
To help brand managers understand consumer dissatisfaction, purchase resistance and brand perception, translating consumer insights into actionable marketing strategies.
# Key Features:
1) Consumer de-influencing insight identification
2) Analysis of purchase resistance and consumer concerns
3) Identification of recurring dissatisfaction themes
4) Consumer sentiment and perception analysis
5) Brand-related insight generation
6) AI-assisted interpretation of consumer behaviour

# Business Value:

By identifying why consumers discourage or resist purchases, the platform can help marketers:

1) Understand potential barriers to product adoption
2) Identify gaps between brand promises and consumer experiences
3) Develop more relevant marketing communication
4) Strengthen consumer trust and brand positioning
5) Discover opportunities for product and experience improvement
